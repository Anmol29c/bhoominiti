Frequently Asked Questions

Understand how the platform supports evidence-based land governance.

Official-source ready
What is BhoomiNiti?

A proposed national digital platform that brings land-governance research, policy evidence, datasets and geospatial indicators into one discoverable workspace.

Who can use the platform?

Researchers, policymakers, government departments, academics, analysts and verified data contributors can use different platform capabilities according to their roles.

How does the AI assistant work?

The production version would use retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) over verified government documents, research papers and approved datasets, with source provenance and confidence indicators.

Is the Policy Simulator an official forecast?

No. The prototype simulator is illustrative. A production model must be calibrated, validated and approved using authoritative datasets and domain expertise.

Where should official land-policy information be verified?

Use the official Department of Land Resources website and its published DILRMP documents, guidelines and reports.

Can state-level land records be connected?

Yes. The proposed architecture can integrate state/UT sources through secure APIs, data-sharing agreements, metadata standards and validation workflows.
